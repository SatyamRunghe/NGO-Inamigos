Who We Are

Established in 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla

InAmigos Foundation is an ISO 9001:2015, 80G & 12A certified NGO. We believe that true change happens when minds unite for a single cause.

From educating underprivileged children to rescuing street animals and empowering women with financial independence, our mission is to reach the unreached and provide them with the tools they need to thrive.

Our Core Initiatives

Discover the programs we run to bring smiles and support to those who need it most.

Project Bachpanshala

Providing basic education, digital literacy, and holistic care to underprivileged children to secure their future.

Project Udaan

Empowering women with vocational skills and financial independence so they can lead the change in their families.

Project Prakriti

Protecting the environment through massive tree plantation drives and promoting eco-friendly sustainability.

Project Seva

Providing food, warm clothes, and basic survival essentials to needy and underprivileged communities.
